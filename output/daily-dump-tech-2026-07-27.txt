Samsung has officially opened preorders for its new foldable lineup, including the Galaxy Z Fold 8, the Z Fold 8 Ultra, and the Z Flip 8. The Fold 8 series moves to a 21-by-9 aspect ratio for the cover display, a departure from the 23.1-by-9 ratio used in the previous generation. The Z Fold 8 Ultra features a titanium frame and a 7.8-inch internal OLED panel, replacing the aluminum construction and 7.6-inch panel found in the prior flagship iteration. Alongside these devices, Samsung launched the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 and the standard Watch 9, both featuring the new three-nanometer processor architecture that increases battery efficiency by twenty percent over the Watch 8 series.

Apple has debuted the first teaser for its upcoming series adaptation of Neuromancer. The project, which is part of Apple's expanding science fiction slate, was presented during San Diego Comic-Con. The series is produced by Apple Studios in collaboration with Skydance Television, with Graham Roland serving as showrunner and writer. The production utilizes a ten-episode format for its first season, drawing directly from the 1984 novel by William Gibson, which serves as the source material for the adaptation.

In the field of artificial intelligence, researchers are exploring the integration of brain wave data for physical AI models. These models are being tested with multimodal inputs, combining camera feeds and annotation data with neural signal readings to improve spatial navigation and physical interaction. Current testing involves non-invasive electroencephalography sensors that track motor-cortex activation patterns to calibrate robotic limb movement. This development follows previous efforts that relied exclusively on tactile feedback and visual recognition algorithms to train humanoid systems for navigation in unstructured environments.

On the legal front, an artist has filed a lawsuit against an AI meme generator platform. The claim alleges that the service used the artist's personal comic work as an ad template without authorization, raising questions regarding the use of private assets within AI training datasets and generation outputs. The complaint, filed in federal court, cites unauthorized reproduction of copyrighted imagery that the platform allegedly ingested from web-scraped archives. The lawsuit specifically identifies nineteen instances where the platform’s generative engine produced variations of the artist’s characters for commercial promotional material without a licensing agreement.

Regarding government regulation, the current administration has established an AI brain trust to formalize national policy. Officials indicate that the group is navigating competing priorities from various stakeholders, including developers, researchers, and economic policy advisors, as they work to define the parameters of federal oversight. The task force is currently reviewing the existing National AI Initiative Act to determine if updates are required to address generative model transparency and data provenance. The committee is composed of fifteen members drawn from federal agencies, including the Department of Commerce and the National Science Foundation, with a mandate to deliver a framework report by the end of the fourth quarter.

These campaigns, often led by wellness influencers, are positioning these products against traditional ADHD stimulants despite a lack of clinical evidence supporting their efficacy.
