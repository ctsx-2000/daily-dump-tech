It is August 05, 2026. This is your Daily Dump of tech news.

SpaceX reported its first quarterly earnings since going public in June, showing a significant shift in revenue composition. The company generated 8.4 billion dollars in total revenue this quarter, effectively doubling the 4.2 billion dollars reported during the same period last year. Its AI infrastructure division, which leases high-density compute clusters powered by the company’s satellite-linked data centers, now generates 2.6 billion dollars. This segment has surpassed the company’s space-related earnings of 2.1 billion dollars, which are derived primarily from satellite launches and government contracts. During the earnings call, leadership announced plans to build a terrestrial mobile network to compete directly with existing major U.S. carriers. This network will utilize the company’s existing orbital constellation to provide backhaul and coverage, aiming to capture a portion of the 200 billion dollar annual U.S. wireless service market.

Apple is preparing to allow clipboard access between iPhones and Windows PCs. This change follows an interoperability request from Microsoft within the European Union, which mandates that large gatekeeper platforms open their ecosystems to third-party hardware under the Digital Markets Act. The integration will utilize a localized encrypted relay to allow users to copy text and images on an iPhone and paste them directly into Windows applications. This functionality replaces the previous ecosystem lock, which restricted cross-device clipboard synchronization exclusively to Apple’s own hardware ecosystem, such as the Handoff feature between iPhone and Mac.

Signal has updated its platform to allow users to link multiple phones to a single account. Previously, users could only link secondary devices like tablets or desktop computers to a primary smartphone, a limitation necessitated by the platform’s end-to-end encryption protocol. The update introduces a multi-device synchronization architecture that permits linking an additional Android device or iPhone to a single phone number. This enables real-time message synchronization across all connected handsets, allowing users to initiate and continue conversations simultaneously on two primary mobile devices without relying on cloud-based backups.

In AI research, a new report from SaferAI highlights a growing performance gap between proprietary frontier models and open-weight alternatives. While models like the newly released GLM 5.2 are approaching frontier-level capabilities in benchmarks like the MMLU and HumanEval, the report notes that these open models lack the standardized safety mitigations found in closed systems. Specifically, the researchers found that open-weight models lack the automated adversarial training layers used by proprietary systems to prevent the generation of restricted or harmful content. This has renewed industry debate regarding the governance of open-weight distributions that allow for unrestricted local inspection and modification, particularly as these models now require only 16 gigabytes of VRAM to operate.

Finally, Lucid Motors has outlined a four-point turnaround plan to address its financial position, targeting 1.4 billion dollars in cost savings. The strategy focuses on the launch of a new mid-size electric vehicle priced at 45,000 dollars, intended to replace the previous high-end luxury-only focus. The plan also includes the completion of a manufacturing facility in Saudi Arabia, which is designed to increase regional production capacity to 150,000 vehicles per year. Broader operational expense reductions involve a 15 percent reduction in headcount, and the company is shifting development resources toward autonomous robotaxi software. This pivot follows a 30 percent decline in quarterly vehicle deliveries compared to the previous year, as the company works to lower its per-unit manufacturing costs by 20 percent.
