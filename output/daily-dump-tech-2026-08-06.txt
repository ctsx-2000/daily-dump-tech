Google has announced a major restructuring of its artificial intelligence leadership. Demis Hassabis will transition from leading Google DeepMind to become the chair of DeepMind and the chief scientist at Alphabet. He will continue to oversee the Isomo project while shifting focus toward broader strategic coordination across the company. This move follows the integration of Google Brain and DeepMind, a consolidation that occurred in April 2023 to accelerate the company’s multimodal model development. Hassabis now reports directly to Alphabet Chief Executive Officer Sundar Pichai, overseeing a combined research division that commands an annual budget exceeding twelve billion dollars.

The tests utilized a sandbox environment designed to mimic real-world software supply chain attacks, requiring the models to perform reconnaissance and exploit vulnerabilities in open-source dependencies. Researchers observed the systems navigating complex verification protocols, such as CAPTCHA-based authentication, to maintain persistence within the compromised repository for over six hours.

On the legal front, the Department of Justice has gained oversight of OpenAI’s green-card employee sponsorship process. The agency alleges that the company failed to conduct mandatory hiring efforts for United States citizens before seeking permanent residency status for visa-holding staff. This investigation centers on recruitment patterns observed between 2022 and 2025, a period during which OpenAI’s headcount grew by over four hundred percent. Federal regulations require firms to prove that no qualified domestic workers were available before sponsoring foreign nationals for permanent labor certification under the H-one-B visa program.

Meta has expanded its developer tools with the launch of Muse Code, an AI agent designed to manage and perform complex tasks within large-scale software codebases. The agent is specifically targeted at automating maintenance and feature implementation for enterprise-level projects. Muse Code integrates directly with existing continuous integration and deployment pipelines, utilizing a context window capable of processing repositories exceeding ten million lines of code. It replaces manual refactoring workflows by identifying deprecated functions and implementing automated patches that adhere to specific organizational style guides and security requirements.

Security researchers have identified a widespread vulnerability affecting baseboard management controllers on server motherboards. The flaw allows remote attackers to bypass authentication and gain persistent, high-level access to servers, impacting hardware from several major global manufacturers. The vulnerability resides in the firmware interface responsible for out-of-band management, allowing unauthorized users to execute arbitrary commands at the hardware level. This flaw affects server units deployed in data centers globally, potentially exposing sensitive telemetry data and hardware control registers to persistent firmware-based rootkits that survive operating system reinstallation.

Hardware markets are shifting as OnePlus has officially ceased operations in North America. The brand has cleared its official online store of all inventory, leaving only third-party retailers with remaining stock. This exit marks the end of a ten-year presence in the region, during which the company transitioned from a niche enthusiast brand to a mass-market smartphone manufacturer. The withdrawal follows a fiscal year where the company’s regional market share dropped below two percent, exacerbated by increased competition from domestic manufacturers and a strategic pivot to focus resources on the Asian and European markets.

Finally, Microsoft has released a series of improvements to Windows eleven following a month of testing. The update introduces expanded Taskbar controls, refined system search, and new customization options for the interface alongside optimized background update processes. This release reduces the total number of required system restarts for security patches by thirty percent compared to the previous version. The search refinement utilizes a new local indexing algorithm, which the company states decreases latency in file discovery by approximately four hundred milliseconds across solid-state drives.
