Google has launched the Pixel eleven series, featuring the base model starting at eight hundred ninety-nine dollars, with Pro and Pro XL variants priced at one thousand ninety-nine and one thousand two hundred ninety-nine dollars respectively. The new lineup includes the Pro Fold, which starts at one thousand eight hundred ninety-nine dollars, all powered by the new Tensor G six chip. The Tensor G six features an upgraded neural processing unit built on a three-nanometer process, which replaces the previous four-nanometer architecture used in the Pixel ten. The base model now ships with twelve gigabytes of RAM as standard, an increase from the eight gigabytes found in the predecessor, while the Pro models feature a new telephoto sensor with five times optical zoom capabilities.

Twitch has introduced an opt-out mechanism for users who do not want their content used to train Amazon artificial intelligence models. The setting allows creators to exclude their streams, video on demand, clips, and chat logs from future dataset ingestion for generative AI. This move follows the recent integration of Amazon Bedrock services into the Twitch infrastructure, which began processing real-time platform data for model tuning last quarter. The opt-out toggle is located within the creator dashboard under the data privacy menu, and once selected, it prevents the platform’s ingestion pipeline from scraping the creator's archived broadcasts for model training sets.

In browser security, Chrome has implemented device-bound session credentials to combat account takeovers. This technology binds authentication cookies to the specific machine where they were generated, rendering stolen session tokens useless if exfiltrated by attackers. Previously, session cookies were portable, allowing threat actors to inject stolen tokens into different browsers to bypass multi-factor authentication. By utilizing hardware-backed cryptographic keys stored within the local machine's Trusted Platform Module, the browser ensures that a session token only functions when the request originates from the original hardware device.

A significant supply-chain attack has resulted in the theft of terabytes of credentials. The breach originated from a compromised AI software package, affecting two thousand five hundred users whose access tokens and login data were scraped and exfiltrated. The malicious package was hosted on a public repository for twelve days before detection, masquerading as a library for natural language processing. The stolen data includes API keys for major cloud service providers and administrative credentials for enterprise-level development environments used by the affected organizations.

In the startup sector, the AI coding company Cognition is in discussions to raise capital at a forty billion dollar valuation. This follows a one billion dollar funding round earlier this year that valued the firm at twenty-six billion dollars. The capital is intended to fund the expansion of their autonomous software engineering agents, which have seen a forty percent increase in monthly active enterprise users since January. This new valuation reflects a total growth of fifty-three percent in market capitalization over the last seven months of fiscal operations.

Northrop Grumman is attempting to extend the operational life of aging satellites using its new mission robotic vehicle. The spacecraft is currently performing a maneuver to attach a new propulsion thruster to an existing satellite, a first for automated in-orbit servicing. The mission vehicle utilizes a proprietary docking interface to physically latch onto the satellite’s exterior structure, replenishing its fuel supply and correcting its orbital trajectory. This capability effectively adds five to seven years of service life to communications satellites that have depleted their onboard propellant reserves.

Grubhub has begun issuing payments to customers and drivers as part of a twenty-four million dollar settlement with the Federal Trade Commission. The payout follows a resolution regarding the company’s business practices and fee structures. The settlement covers claims related to undisclosed service fees and the misclassification of delivery zones that impacted consumer pricing between twenty-twenty and twenty-twenty-four. Individual payments are being distributed based on transaction volume during the period of the alleged violations, with the average customer receiving approximately forty-two dollars.

Finally, Meta is donating fifteen thousand pairs of its Ray-Ban smart glasses to an Irish charity. The distribution is intended for use by blind and visually impaired individuals to assist with navigation and object identification through the device's integrated artificial intelligence features. The glasses use an onboard camera and multimodal large language model to narrate visual surroundings and read text aloud in real time. This donation represents the largest single corporate deployment of augmented reality hardware for accessibility purposes to date.
