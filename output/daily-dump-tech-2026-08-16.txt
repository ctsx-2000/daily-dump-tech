A critical vulnerability in the macOS screen sharing utility is currently under active exploitation. The flaw allows remote attackers to bypass password authentication and gain full control of affected machines. Security researchers identified the exploit as targeting the Screen Sharing agent, which is a core component of the macOS Remote Management framework. The vulnerability specifically affects systems running macOS Sequoia and Sonoma, with confirmed incidents involving unauthorized access to administrative sessions without prior authentication credentials. This bypasses the standard handshake protocol that typically requires a verified user password or an approved identity certificate.

In AI development, Anthropic has released technical specifications for its new model watermarking system. The documentation details how these digital signatures are embedded within generated text and code, including how the watermarks persist through common editing and formatting changes. The system utilizes a statistical approach to signal insertion, embedding low-entropy patterns into token sequences during the inference phase. These patterns remain detectable even after a model output has been modified by up to twenty percent of its original character count or reformatted into different document structures. This allows for verification of origin across disparate platforms and interfaces that utilize the underlying model architecture.

Google is expanding customization options for mobile devices with the latest Android 17 QPR2 beta. The update introduces a broader range of dynamic color theming, allowing the system interface to generate more complex palettes based on user wallpaper selections. This version increases the algorithm’s color extraction limit from five to twelve distinct hues, which are then applied to system-wide UI elements including the notification shade, lock screen clock, and app icon accents. The update replaces the previous color extraction engine, which was limited to monochromatic or primary-color matching, with a multi-layered contrast mapping system that maintains accessibility standards for text readability regardless of the base wallpaper’s brightness or saturation levels.

On the financial front, negotiations are ongoing regarding the potential sale of PayPal. The company is currently in talks with Stripe and the private equity firm Advent as part of a broader effort to restructure its business operations. PayPal’s current market capitalization stands at approximately 68 billion dollars, down from its 2021 peak valuation of over 300 billion dollars. Reports from financial analysts indicate that the acquisition discussions involve a premium on current trading prices, with a focus on integrating PayPal’s massive consumer transaction database with Stripe’s B2B payment infrastructure. The potential divestiture follows three consecutive quarters of declining transaction margins and an aggressive push to reduce operational overhead by fifteen percent.

Investment in fusion energy remains concentrated among a small group of industry players. Data shows that out of the seven-point-one billion dollars raised by fusion startups to date, the vast majority has been funneled to fewer than a dozen companies. This concentration of capital follows a trend where three private firms account for over four billion dollars of the total funding pool. The majority of these investments are directed toward magnetic confinement fusion and laser-driven inertial confinement systems, leaving early-stage alternative designs with less than five percent of the total available capital.

Turning to energy infrastructure, new data on United States power generation shows that utility-scale solar projects now lead all other new capacity additions this year. Battery storage installations occupy the second spot, while fossil fuel project deployments have declined significantly. Solar accounts for 58 percent of all new gigawatts added to the grid in the first two quarters of 2026, representing a 12 percent increase over the same period last year. Conversely, new natural gas and coal capacity represents less than 8 percent of new grid connections, marking the lowest contribution from thermal power plants since federal record-keeping began in 1990.

Finally, a joint venture has completed the first test flight of a large-scale all-electric aircraft. The flight required five dollars worth of electricity, as the project aims to establish the feasibility of hybrid-electric commercial aviation. The craft, a modified short-haul regional jet, utilized a high-density lithium-sulfur battery array to power two electric motors during a forty-minute cruise at an altitude of ten thousand feet. This mission consumed approximately eighty-five kilowatt-hours of power to complete a ninety-mile circuit, replacing the four hundred gallons of jet fuel typically required for an equivalent flight profile.
