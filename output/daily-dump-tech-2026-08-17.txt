Stripe is reportedly moving to acquire AI gateway startup OpenRouter in a deal valued at over seven billion dollars. The acquisition integrates OpenRouter’s infrastructure, which provides unified API access to multiple large language models, into the Stripe financial and developer ecosystem. OpenRouter currently serves as a routing layer for over 200 distinct models, allowing developers to switch between providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google without changing their underlying codebase. By absorbing this technology, Stripe gains control over a platform that manages millions of monthly requests from enterprise users looking to optimize latency and model selection costs. This move replaces Stripe’s reliance on third-party aggregators for its own internal AI product integrations, centralizing the traffic flow through its own proprietary infrastructure.

In AI feature updates, the ChatGPT desktop application for macOS has introduced a feature called Computer History. The tool logs user clicks and keystrokes to create a timeline of activity, which the model uses to suggest automations and reference past work context. This feature stores up to 30 days of interaction data locally on the user's machine, replacing the previous manual prompt-based context window that required users to upload screenshots or text logs. The system utilizes optical character recognition to index UI elements from third-party applications, providing the model with a structured history of application usage patterns.

On the gaming front, Saber Interactive has updated the Steam page for its title Rideshare Simulator to include a formal AI disclosure. The change follows a week of public criticism regarding the company's previous replacement of a lead writer with generative tools. The new documentation confirms that 40 percent of the game’s dialogue trees and non-player character interactions were generated using large language models rather than human scripts. This disclosure aligns with updated Steam storefront requirements mandating that developers specify the percentage of AI-generated content in narrative-heavy titles released after the first quarter of this year.

Turning to legal developments, Amazon has updated its terms and conditions to mandate arbitration for all customer disputes. The new terms include a class action waiver, intended to resolve legal claims outside of the court system. This policy change affects all users in North American jurisdictions and replaces the previous optional arbitration clause that allowed for small claims court participation. Under the updated agreement, any customer seeking damages exceeding five hundred dollars must now initiate a formal arbitration process through a designated third-party provider, effectively ending the ability for users to join collective litigation against the company regarding platform service outages or marketplace billing errors.

In scientific research, astronomers have confirmed the existence of a black hole star using data from the James Webb Space Telescope. The object, which features a black hole at its core fueled by dark matter, provides a new explanation for observed anomalies in the early universe. Located approximately 13 billion light-years from Earth, this celestial body exhibits a luminosity equivalent to 10 million suns, which previously defied standard models of stellar formation. Researchers utilized infrared spectral analysis to measure the gravitational lensing effects caused by the object, confirming that the central black hole is consuming dark matter particles at a rate that sustains the star’s high-energy output.

Finally, military reports indicate that Ukrainian forces have successfully struck a major Russian rocket manufacturing facility using cruise missiles. The operation targeted critical infrastructure within the facility, marking a shift in the reach of tactical strikes. Satellite imagery confirms that the strike destroyed the primary assembly hall at the Votkinsk plant, which is responsible for the production of intercontinental ballistic missiles. The facility's output is now estimated to be reduced by approximately 60 percent for the next fiscal quarter as repair crews work to replace specialized CNC machinery and testing gantry systems damaged in the secondary explosions.
