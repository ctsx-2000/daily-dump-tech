Apple has increased the monthly price of its streaming service to fourteen dollars and ninety-nine cents, marking its fourth price hike in four years. This represents a fifty percent increase from the initial launch price of nine dollars and ninety-nine cents in 2022. The annual subscription fee has risen to one hundred and nineteen dollars, a twenty-dollar increase from the previous rate. The company has also adjusted the pricing for its individual Apple One bundles, which now start at twenty-two dollars and ninety-five cents for the entry-level tier, representing a twelve percent increase across all plan segments.

Andreessen Horowitz is launching a one-point-one billion dollar fund titled Machine Age. The firm is shifting focus from pure software investments to the physical infrastructure and hardware required for large-scale artificial intelligence operations. This capital allocation target includes investments in power grid upgrades, specialized cooling systems for high-density compute environments, and domestic semiconductor manufacturing facilities. The fund represents a departure from the firm’s previous three-hundred-million-dollar AI infrastructure vehicle, signaling a move toward capital-intensive industrial assets rather than consumer-facing software applications.

Meta is testing autonomous robots within its data centers to perform physical maintenance tasks. The machines are designed to manage cable swaps and server resets, with the company aiming to automate routine hardware labor currently handled by technicians. These robots utilize high-resolution vision systems to navigate aisles and identify failed hardware components without human intervention. The deployment follows a pilot program that successfully demonstrated a forty percent reduction in the time required to restore connectivity for offline server racks.

In AI research, an Anthropic scientist has demonstrated a system capable of self-improvement. The model identified and corrected its own misaligned behaviors across ten benchmarks without suffering performance degradation in other areas. The system utilized a recursive training loop to adjust its own internal weights based on outputs that previously failed to meet safety guidelines. This testing occurred across a training set of three hundred million parameters, showing a consistent reduction in bias markers compared to the base model architecture.

Google is implementing stricter memory usage limits for applications on the Android platform. The change is intended to mitigate hardware resource constraints as increased data center demand for AI chips continues to impact supply chains for lower-cost mobile devices. The new policy mandates that background applications remain under a five-hundred-megabyte active memory threshold, a reduction from the previous one-gigabyte limit. This shift directly impacts devices with six gigabytes of RAM or less, forcing aggressive process termination for background tasks to prioritize active foreground operations.

Hardware startup Plaud has introduced a set of wireless earbuds equipped with four G connectivity. The device allows users to record audio and interact with artificial intelligence assistants directly without a tethered smartphone connection. The earbuds feature an integrated eSIM and a battery rated for six hours of continuous cellular data transmission. This hardware replaces the need for a secondary recording device or a smartphone bridge, utilizing a dedicated onboard neural processing unit to handle speech-to-text transcription locally before offloading data to the cloud.

The Environmental Protection Agency is moving to exempt data centers from specific public notification requirements regarding air pollution. This policy change would remove the obligation for operators to provide a public comment period when establishing new facilities, amid increasing community pushback over site environmental impact. The exemption applies to backup diesel generators used at sites exceeding fifty megawatts of capacity, which are currently classified as stationary emission sources. The proposal removes the mandate for local air quality impact reports to be filed sixty days prior to construction permits being granted.

Finally, authorities have arrested two individuals suspected of belonging to the hacking collective known as TeamPCP. The group is accused of conducting a widespread supply-chain attack campaign that compromised over one thousand organizations. Federal indictments indicate the suspects utilized stolen credentials to gain administrative access to third-party software update servers. This access allowed the group to inject malicious scripts into legitimate software packages, which were then distributed to the internal networks of target companies across the logistics and financial sectors.
