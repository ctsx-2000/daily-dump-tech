It is September 10, 2026. This is your Daily Dump of tech news.

Automattic’s board of directors has forced CEO Matt Mullenweg into a leave of absence effective immediately. The decision follows a series of public disputes regarding the company’s commercial licensing strategies and the management of the WordPress ecosystem. The board has appointed a transition committee to oversee day-to-day operations while a search for an interim successor begins. This leadership change marks the first time in the company’s twenty-one-year history that the founder has been removed from active management.

Apple today announced the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, featuring new sensors capable of transcribing and summarizing ambient conversations. These devices utilize a dedicated neural processing unit that processes audio locally rather than transmitting raw data to external servers. The company confirmed that users will face daily usage limits for these artificial intelligence features, with expanded access available through future subscription tiers. The Series 12 replaces the Series 11, introducing a redesigned display panel that increases power efficiency by fifteen percent compared to the prior model.

In other hardware news, Volvo is updating its XC40 hybrid model with a refreshed interior and the integration of Google’s Gemini AI as the primary infotainment assistant. The system replaces the previous voice-command interface, providing direct control over vehicle climate, navigation, and entertainment settings. The vehicle will also include a new sensor array, featuring high-resolution LiDAR and external cameras, for improved safety monitoring when it reaches dealerships early next year. This hardware update marks the first integration of this specific AI architecture into the company’s compact SUV lineup.

Google has patched two hundred and thirty security vulnerabilities in its latest Chrome update, including a zero-day exploit that is currently being used in active attacks. This is the seventh zero-day vulnerability patched in the browser since the beginning of the year, representing a thirty percent increase in reported critical exploits compared to the same period in 2025. The update addresses memory corruption issues in the browser engine that have been identified in targeted campaigns against high-profile users.

On the security front, attackers are actively breaching F5 Big-IP access policy manager devices to install Linux rootkits. The malware intercepts PHP file loading to inject a fileless web shell directly into system memory, avoiding disk-based detection. Security researchers report that the campaign specifically targets unpatched enterprise gateways, with over four thousand instances identified as compromised across global networks in the last forty-eight hours.

Microsoft has broken its Patch Tuesday record with a single-day release of nine hundred and seventy-four unique vulnerability patches. The massive update addresses security flaws across the Windows ecosystem, including remote code execution vulnerabilities in the kernel, and includes critical fixes from Adobe covering the Creative Cloud suite. This release cycle surpasses the previous record of eight hundred and twelve patches set in May 2025, and marks the largest deployment of security updates in the company's history.

Blizzard Entertainment employees have ratified their first union contract with parent company Microsoft. The agreement follows prolonged negotiations and covers the terms of employment for nearly nineteen hundred staff members across development, quality assurance, and administrative departments. The contract establishes new minimum salary tiers, standardized overtime compensation, and updated severance guidelines for staff impacted by future project cancellations or studio restructuring.

Finally, the video game Valheim has officially launched version one point zero after five years in early access. The title sustained a significant player base throughout its development period, consistently maintaining over twenty thousand daily concurrent users on Steam and peaking at over five hundred thousand users during its initial release phase. The final update adds the remaining three biomes, a complete overhaul of the crafting progression system, and support for dedicated cross-platform server hosting.
