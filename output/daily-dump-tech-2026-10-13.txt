It is October 13, 2026. This is your Daily Dump of tech news.

Apple has released the HomeView smart display, priced at two hundred ninety-nine dollars. The unit features an eight-inch liquid retina touchscreen and is powered by the A18 Bionic chip to support localized processing for home automation commands. The device acts as a centralized hub for Matter-enabled smart home devices, replacing the functionality previously handled by the HomePod and Apple TV as the primary interface for the Home app. The unit is available in two configurations, including a wall-mounted version that requires a separate mounting kit priced at fifty-nine dollars.

Alongside that release, the company launched the second-generation HomePod Mini. Retailing for one hundred forty-nine dollars, the device features the S10 processor, which provides a forty percent increase in computational audio speed over the original S5 chip found in the first-generation model. The new hardware includes a built-in U3 ultra-wideband chip for precise proximity handoff and is scheduled to begin shipping on October thirtieth.

Google has expanded access to its latest AI model, Gemini Four Argon. The update is now available to users within the primary Gemini application and via the AI Mode integrated into Google Search. This model replaces the Gemini Three series, offering a thirty percent reduction in latency for text-based queries and a twenty-five percent improvement in reasoning benchmarks as reported in the latest technical white paper. The model supports a two-million-token context window, allowing for the ingestion of entire technical manuals or multi-hour video files for analysis.

In the gaming industry, Epic Games has transitioned to an AI-first development mandate for its upcoming Disney project. The team is utilizing tools including Anthropic's Claude model to generate both code and assets for the title, which has been delayed from its original March launch window to the second quarter of next year. The mandate shifts the production workflow from traditional manual asset creation to a generative process, with human personnel limited to oversight and quality assurance reviews. Current reporting indicates that the studio has reduced its external contract workforce by fifteen percent as a result of this transition.

Bumble has agreed to be acquired in a deal valued at one point three billion dollars. The acquisition follows a period of declining subscriber counts, which dropped by twelve percent year-over-year in the most recent fiscal quarter, alongside a nine percent decrease in total revenue. The buyer is an undisclosed private equity firm that also maintains stakes in other social discovery platforms. The transaction is expected to finalize pending regulatory approval in the first quarter of 2027.

SpaceX has introduced a new subscription tier titled SpaceXSI. The bundle combines access to X Premium, the Grok AI chatbot, and the Cursor code editor, with plans ranging from eight dollars to two hundred dollars per month depending on the level of API access and compute credits included. This offering marks the first time SpaceX has bundled software services from the broader Elon Musk corporate portfolio directly into a single billing mechanism for enterprise and individual accounts.

Streaming service Discovery Plus is increasing its subscription costs. The ad-supported tier will rise to six dollars and ninety-nine cents per month, while the ad-free version will increase to eleven dollars and ninety-nine cents. This represents a fifteen percent price hike for the ad-supported tier and a ten percent increase for the premium version. The company cited rising content licensing fees and infrastructure costs associated with maintaining its global content distribution network as the primary drivers for these adjustments.

Finally, security researchers have identified an unsoundness bug within the Rust programming language borrow checker that has persisted for eleven years. The issue pertains to how the compiler handles specific memory references, potentially allowing for data races in multi-threaded environments that the borrow checker was intended to prevent. The vulnerability is currently being tracked in the project's public issue database, with maintainers noting that the fix requires a fundamental modification to the compiler's primary type system logic.
