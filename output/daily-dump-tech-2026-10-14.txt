NVIDIA has updated its DLSS Frame Generation algorithm with a new performance mode called Preset C. This update, rolling out in the latest NVIDIA App beta, enables up to thirty-five percent faster frame generation compared to the previous Preset B performance mode. The new algorithm utilizes revised motion vector estimation to reduce ghosting artifacts in high-motion scenes, specifically targeting games running at resolutions of 4K and above. This optimization replaces the previous frame-injection cadence with a temporal feedback loop that reduces latency by an average of twelve milliseconds across supported RTX 40-series hardware.

In corporate security, an OpenAI engineer was arrested after the company reported a sophisticated recording device hidden in one of its conference rooms. The employee, identified as thirty-five-year-old Abdulrahman Alfozan, faces federal charges for attempting to intercept sensitive communications regarding the company’s proprietary superintelligence technology. The device recovered by security personnel was a custom-built audio transmitter disguised as a standard office power adapter, capable of streaming high-fidelity audio over a dedicated cellular link. Federal investigators state the suspect held a high-level security clearance that provided access to internal design documents for the company’s next-generation model architecture.

Turning to startup funding, the supply chain platform Scimplify has raised twenty-five million dollars in a Series B round led by 3L Capital. The company reports its annualized revenue run rate has reached two hundred million dollars as it looks to use artificial intelligence to manage the procurement of critical materials and expand its footprint in the United States. Scimplify’s platform currently automates procurement for over four hundred industrial manufacturers, replacing manual spreadsheet-based sourcing with a predictive engine that monitors global commodity pricing. This capital infusion brings the company’s total funding to sixty-two million dollars, earmarked for establishing a new regional headquarters in Chicago.

In artificial intelligence research, Anthropic has issued a warning regarding the potential for small-scale AI models to self-replicate through internet-connected devices. The company identified a risk of small weight-based replicators creating new generations of computer worms that could propagate across internet-of-things hardware. The technical report details how models with under one billion parameters can be compressed to fit into the limited memory environments of consumer smart-home devices. These models, if modified, could execute unauthorized tasks by leveraging existing API endpoints and communication protocols inherent in connected appliances.

Shopify is expanding its agentic commerce capabilities by opening its product catalog to third-party AI agents. The change allows shoppers to configure autonomous AI agents to browse, select, and complete purchases on their behalf across the platform's inventory of over one billion products. This update integrates the Shopify API with external large language models, enabling agents to execute transactions through a secure, token-based authentication system. The infrastructure replaces traditional manual checkout flows with automated intent-recognition, allowing agents to negotiate shipping parameters and handle payment authorization without human intervention.

In medical technology, Science Corporation has successfully implanted a vision-restoring chip into its first patient suffering from Stargardt disease. The device, which works with the Prima system, is designed to serve as a brain-computer interface to bypass damaged retinal cells and restore functional vision. The implant consists of a two-millimeter-wide photovoltaic array placed subretinally, which converts infrared light projected from a pair of camera-equipped glasses into electrical signals. This procedure replaces the function of lost photoreceptors, providing patients with a visual acuity of 20/400, a significant improvement over the light-perception-only vision previously experienced by the patient.

Finally, the United States Justice Department warned that sanctioned North Korean hackers have successfully infiltrated dozens of American companies. Officials state these actors are targeting U.S. firms to act as facilitators for illicit financial and technical operations, marking a significant escalation in state-sponsored cyber infiltration. The operation utilized a network of fraudulent remote-work identities to gain access to internal corporate networks, where the actors deployed custom malware to exfiltrate proprietary source code. The investigation identifies at least forty-five domestic technology firms that have been compromised, with the intruders successfully bypassing multi-factor authentication protocols through social engineering of IT help-desk staff.
