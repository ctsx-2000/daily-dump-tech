The United States Department of Homeland Security has ordered all federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek products and third-party services built on its technology from government systems. The directive follows national security concerns regarding the Chinese-developed artificial intelligence models. This mandate requires agencies to audit all software procurement contracts and purge any integrated AI architectures that rely on DeepSeek’s proprietary training data or API endpoints. The order replaces existing software usage policies by establishing a hard prohibition on any vendor that utilizes DeepSeek as a foundational model for government-facing tools.

In audio streaming, Spotify is rolling out paid podcast memberships in thirty-four regions. The new feature allows creators to offer exclusive content, early access, and ad-free listening while retaining ninety percent of the revenue generated through the platform. This payout structure represents a significant shift from the standard industry royalty model, where creators typically receive between fifty and seventy percent of gross subscription income. The expansion targets a base of over six hundred million monthly active users, allowing individual podcasters to set their own tiered pricing models for listener support.

Regarding platform oversight, court records obtained by news organizations indicate that TikTok employees repeatedly warned executives about child safety risks on the platform. Staffers reported frustration over management failing to act on concerns, specifically regarding minors bypassing existing age restrictions. Internal communications reveal that internal trust and safety teams flagged specific loopholes in the age-verification process that allowed users under the age of thirteen to access adult-oriented content. These records are part of ongoing litigation involving attorneys general from fourteen states who allege that the platform intentionally designed features to maximize the duration of time minors spend on the application.

Adobe has updated the mobile version of Lightroom to include a generative artificial intelligence tool capable of opening closed eyes in photographs. The feature automatically detects subjects with closed eyes and generates an adjustment with a single tap for iOS, iPadOS, and Android users. This tool uses a latent diffusion model trained on millions of portraits to reconstruct facial features based on skeletal structure and surrounding pixel data. The update replaces manual cloning and healing brush methods that previously required hours of professional photo editing to achieve similar results.

Spotify has also confirmed the removal of over fifty million tracks from its recommendation system over the past year. The company categorized the content as AI-generated spam and has modified its discovery algorithms to prioritize music produced by human artists. This purge represents nearly five percent of the platform’s total library, targeting automated uploads that utilized bot-driven streaming counts to dilute the royalty pool. The new algorithmic filtering process identifies acoustic patterns associated with synthetic generation, effectively de-indexing content that lacks verifiable human recording metadata.

In the startup sector, the aviation firm Reliable Robotics has raised one hundred fifteen million dollars in equity and twenty-five million in debt, bringing its valuation above one billion dollars. The company is developing autonomous flight technology and systems for commercial aviation. This funding round supports the certification process for the company’s flight management system, which is designed to automate the taxi, takeoff, and landing phases for mid-sized cargo aircraft. The company currently holds a contract with the United States Air Force to explore the automation of larger military transport planes.

Turning to space exploration, Japan has initiated a mission to retrieve samples from the Martian moon Phobos. The spacecraft is scheduled to attempt landings on the moon in twenty-twenty-nine to collect material for return to Earth. The mission utilizes a propulsion system that relies on solar-electric ion thrusters, allowing for a three-year transit time to the Mars system. The primary scientific objective is to determine whether Phobos is a captured asteroid or a remnant of a larger impact event on the Martian surface, with the sample return capsule planned to touch down in the Australian desert.

Finally, a Russian spacecraft has performed maneuvers in extreme proximity to a satellite utilized by Ukraine. The Russian vessel, Kosmos twenty-six-twelve, moved from hundreds of kilometers away to an immediate orbital position alongside the ICEYE-X-thirty-six satellite. The ICEYE vessel provides high-resolution synthetic aperture radar imagery used for mapping terrain and monitoring infrastructure.
