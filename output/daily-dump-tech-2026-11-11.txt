It is November 11, 2026. This is your Daily Dump of tech news.

OpenAI has reached a ten billion dollar quarterly revenue run rate, marking a fifty percent increase from the six point seven billion dollars recorded in the previous quarter. The company continues to see rapid growth in its enterprise and API segments, which now account for seventy-two percent of total revenue, up from sixty-four percent in the prior quarter. This growth follows the recent release of the Orion model, which has seen adoption across forty-five percent of the Fortune 500. Enterprise usage has specifically spiked among financial services firms, which currently represent the largest single sector by volume for API consumption.

In research, Google DeepMind has launched AlphaGenome 2, an AI model designed to predict how genetic variants affect cellular function. The system identifies the biological roots of diseases by modeling DNA changes at a molecular scale, improving predictive accuracy for protein-coding mutations by thirty-four percent over the previous version released eighteen months ago. It incorporates data from the Human Genome Project and six petabytes of proprietary clinical trial records to simulate how specific amino acid substitutions alter cellular protein folding.

Staying with Google, researchers have utilized AI to mathematically verify critical components of the Signal messaging protocol. The project successfully completed formal verification of key codebases, proving that the implementation maintains end-to-end encryption standards without memory corruption vulnerabilities. This automated verification process required only six hours of compute time, compared to the four months of manual labor previously needed by human security auditors to verify the same cryptographic modules.

On the infrastructure front, Apple experienced an outage today affecting iCloud services, including Mail, Contacts, Calendar, and Game Center. The service disruption lasted five hours and twenty-two minutes, impacting an estimated eighty-five million active users across North America and Europe. System status logs indicate the failure originated in a primary database cluster responsible for authentication tokens, which failed to synchronize during a scheduled load balancer update.

Microsoft is expanding its Auto Super Resolution feature to the Xbox Ally X, the Xbox Ally X-20, and specific Windows Copilot Plus PCs. This feature uses a dedicated neural processing unit to upscale graphics, replacing traditional software-based temporal upscaling methods that previously consumed fifteen percent more system overhead. The implementation on these handheld devices enables 1440p output at a stable sixty frames per second while maintaining the same thermal envelope as previous 1080p native configurations.

In the gaming industry, Bandai Namco is closing Reflector Entertainment, the studio behind the game Unknown Nine. The company will report a twenty-one million euro loss for the third quarter as a direct result of the closure and associated asset write-downs. This decision follows the game's commercial performance, which recorded two hundred thousand units sold globally since its release six weeks ago, falling significantly short of the internal sales target of one point two million units.

Finally, Sony has canceled Fairgames, a live-service shooter currently in development at Haven Studios. This decision follows a series of shifts in Sony’s strategy for its live-service gaming portfolio, which has now seen the cancellation of five out of twelve internally developed live-service titles announced since 2022. The cancellation results in a restructuring at Haven Studios, affecting one hundred and fifty employees as the studio pivots to single-player narrative development.
